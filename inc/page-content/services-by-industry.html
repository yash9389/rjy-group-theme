Our Services

RJY Group offers six integrated services. Every service is available across all five industries.

1. Building Automation Systems (BAS)

Smarter building performance. Design, install, optimize, and maintain connected controls that make facilities easier to operate and more efficient.

  • Controls integration and upgrades
  • System commissioning and optimization
  • Remote monitoring and diagnostics

2. Cyber Security for OT and ICS

Protect operational continuity. Secure the connected building systems that keep facilities safe, productive, and online.

  • OT and ICS risk assessments
  • Network segmentation guidance
  • Secure controls lifecycle support

3. HVAC Systems

Reliability in every season. Preventive maintenance, repair, and performance services for complex HVAC and chiller systems.

  • Preventive and predictive maintenance
  • Chiller and central plant service
  • Efficiency and reliability improvements

4. Generator Services

Standby power, ready when needed. Proactive maintenance, testing, and repair programs for mission-critical standby power systems.

  • Load-bank testing coordination
  • Preventive maintenance
  • Emergency diagnostics and repair

5. Support Staffing

Qualified people, ready to perform. Flexible facilities staffing that closes capability gaps and protects service levels.

  • Skilled technical personnel
  • Short-term and sustained coverage
  • Site-specific onboarding

6. Water Treatment

Protect systems and water quality. Cooling tower and closed-loop programs that reduce corrosion, scale, energy waste, and compliance risk.

  • Closed-loop water treatment
  • Cooling tower programs
  • Testing, reporting, and optimization
